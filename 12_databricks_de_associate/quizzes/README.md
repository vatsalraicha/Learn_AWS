## ⚠️ The 80% pass mark is a high bar

The exam was raised from 70% to **80%** in the July 25, 2025 refresh. To pass safely (i.e., with margin against bad-luck questions), you want to sustain **85%+** on practice quizzes before booking.

- Hitting 70-79% on these quizzes? **You will likely fail the real exam.** Revisit the weakest domain's modules.
- Hitting 80-84%? **Borderline.** Book with caution, or do one more round of weak-area review.
- Hitting 85%+ consistently across all five quizzes? **Book the exam.**

## Question categories

Every quiz mixes four cognitive levels:

- **Recall** — what is X? (direct from FACTS.md)
- **Apply** — given this scenario, which command / option / mode?
- **Diagnose** — given this symptom, what's the cause / fix?
- **Defend** — which of these answers is best for this scenario, and why are the others wrong?

The live exam is dominated by Apply and Diagnose. Recall is ~20% of the exam at most.

## How to use

1. Block 30-45 minutes per quiz. No distractions.
2. Read each question. Pick one answer. Write it down.
3. Do NOT peek at the answer block until done.
4. Score yourself. Re-read the module for any wrong answer.
5. After all five quizzes, take **all five back-to-back as a simulated 90-minute exam**.
